    What's inside the plug I'm designing

    This is my plan for ElecTrack's smart plug. It will sit between the wall and one appliance and report how much power it uses. Scroll to open it up, front to back.

    1. Front shell

      The appliance will plug into a standard Philippine outlet. A ring at the corner is planned to glow while the plug is sending readings.

    2. PZEM-004T v3 meter

      A current transformer coil and metering chip will measure voltage, current, watts and kWh on the live line.

    3. ESP32 board

      The firmware I'm planning will poll the meter over UART every few seconds and post the readings over Wi‑Fi to our backend.

    4. Back shell and prongs

      It plugs straight into the wall. We scoped it to monitor only, so there's no remote switch.
